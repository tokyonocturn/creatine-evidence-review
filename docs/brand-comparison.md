## 3. What certification buys

Certification is the main quality differentiator that is visible before purchase.

- **NSF Certified for Sport** and **Informed Sport** screen for about 285–290 banned
  substances, review labels and facilities, and monitor ongoing production. They are
  the right choice for drug-tested athletes [R49; R51].
- **Informed Choice** is retail monitoring, not batch-by-batch release testing
  [R52].
- **NSF Contents Certified** verifies content and contaminants but is not a
  sport-banned-substance programme.

Certification does **not** predict efficacy, and the absence of certification does
not prove a product is contaminated. It is a risk-reduction tool.

## 5. Red flags

- **Proprietary blends** that hide how much creatine is present.
- **"HCl" or "buffered" premiums** with no matched-dose trial.
- **Gummies with less than 5 g per serving** and no certification.
- **Creatine "loading" products** that are just repackaged monohydrate at 3× the
  price.
- **Liquid creatine** with a long shelf life — stability is poor.
- **Heavy-metal claims without a COA or certification**.
- **Prices far below market** from marketplace sellers with no lot traceability.

## 6. Cost per year, and what it means

At 5 g/day:

- $0.15 per 5 g = **$54.70/year**
- $0.33 per 5 g = **$120.45/year**
- $0.49 per 5 g = **$178.44/year**
- $2.22 per 5 g = **$811.11/year**
- $3.12 per 5 g = **$1,138.86/year**

The difference between the cheapest and most expensive product is roughly **$1,084
per year** for the same expected training effect. That is the single most useful
number in this repository.

## 7. Price caveats

- Prices are US one-time retail snapshots from 2026-11-06 to 2026-11-17.
- Subscription, coupon, and marketplace prices are excluded.
- Pack sizes and formulations change; re-check the source URL before buying.
- The sample is purposive, not a census. A brand not listed is not thereby bad.
- Certification status was checked against the certifier's public listing where
  available; "None documented" means no listing was found, not proof of absence.
